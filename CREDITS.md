## Software

[Three.js r170](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/r170), MIT license. A local copy is included in assets/three.module.js, with the complete license in assets/THREE-LICENSE.txt.

The room geometry, procedural textures, camera motion and retro postprocessing shader are authored for this site. The shader is an interpretation of retro game rendering, not a copy of another game's shader.
